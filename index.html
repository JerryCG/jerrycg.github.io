About

Background

I am an incoming PhD student in Political Science at UC San Diego. I explore interdisciplinary questions at the intersection of policy-driven economics and political science, with a focus on how quantitative and empirical methods can speak clearly to theory and policy.

I hold an MA in Computational Social Science (Economics track) from the University of Chicago and a First Class Honors BBA in Finance from City University of Hong Kong, where I ranked first in the Finance major and minored in Computer Science and Mathematics.
