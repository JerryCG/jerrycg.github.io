About

Background

I am a predoctoral researcher working with Hanwei Huang and collaborators on international trade and quantitative macro topics, and I also assist David Y. Yang at Harvard on projects related to trade and international power.

I hold an MA in Computational Social Science (Economics track) from the University of Chicago and a First Class Honors BBA in Finance from City University of Hong Kong, where I ranked first in the Finance major and minored in Computer Science and Mathematics.

My interests sit at the intersection of empirical strategy, structural modeling, and computational methods — especially questions where theory and data can speak to each other clearly.
